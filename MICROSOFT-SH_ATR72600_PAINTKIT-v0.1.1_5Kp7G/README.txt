LOD4 and LOD5 Texture Note

The LOD4 and LOD5 textures are the same texture layout, just at different sizes.

To create the LOD5 texture:

1. Open your completed LOD4 texture.
2. Downsize the LOD4 texture to 256 x 256.
3. Export the downsized texture.
4. Use it to override or replace the LOD5 texture.

This should allow your LOD5 texture to match your LOD4 work while using the correct smaller size.
